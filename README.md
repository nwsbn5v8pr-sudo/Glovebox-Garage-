# Glovebox Garage

Oil change, tire rotation, registration and service reminders for all your vehicles, in one simple phone app.

**Open the app:** https://nwsbn5v8pr-sudo.github.io/Glovebox-Garage-/

- Works in any phone browser and installs to your Home Screen (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen; Android: Install).
- Private: no account, no ads. Your vehicles are saved only on your device. Back up from Settings.
- Works offline. The optional VIN and recall lookups use the U.S. government's free NHTSA services and send only the VIN or year/make/model.
